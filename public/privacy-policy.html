2.4 Referral Programme Data

8. Cookies and Tracking

8.1 What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. Similar technologies, such as browser local storage, work in the same way and are covered by this section. We use them to keep you signed in, keep the site secure, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used.

8.2 Cookies We Set Without Asking

These are strictly necessary for the site to work, so we do not need your consent for them.

  • Authentication and security: keep you signed in and protect your account and our infrastructure. Set by us, by Firebase (Google) and by Cloudflare.
  • Your cookie choice: we store your answer to the consent banner in your browser's local storage under nr_analytics_consent, so we do not ask you again on every page.

8.3 Cookies We Set Only If You Agree

None of the following run until you select Accept on our cookie banner. If you select Decline, they are never loaded and no analytics or marketing cookies are placed on your device.

  • PostHog (product analytics, hosted in the EU). Records page views, clicks and error reports so we can see which parts of the site work and which do not. Stored in your browser's local storage rather than as cookies, and retained for up to 12 months.
  • HubSpot (analytics and customer relationship management, hosted in the EU). Recognises returning visitors and links website activity to your record if you later contact us or complete a HubSpot form. Sets __hstc and hubspotutk (expire after 6 months), __hssc (expires after 30 minutes of inactivity) and __hssrc (expires when you close your browser).
  • Firebase Analytics (Google). Usage analytics within the NoResume mobile apps and signed-in areas. Data is aggregated and, where possible, anonymised.

We do not use cookies to serve advertising, and we do not sell the data these cookies collect.

8.4 Changing Your Mind

You can withdraw your consent at any time, and it is as easy as giving it. Reopen your cookie choices and select Decline. Any analytics already running in that page will stop and clear its cookies, and nothing will load on your next visit.

8.5 Managing Cookies In Your Browser

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the platform working.
